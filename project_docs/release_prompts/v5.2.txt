DEFINITION OF DONE
==================

Collector Companion can take captured photos, extract OCR information, generate explainable identification candidates, compare them against collection context, and present reviewable suggestions without automatically changing collection data.

This completes the first end-to-end field workflow:

Capture
↓
OCR
↓
Identify
↓
Review

while preserving collector control.
