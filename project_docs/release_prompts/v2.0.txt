## GOAL

Build Collector Operating System v2.0.

This is NOT a major feature expansion release.

This is a consolidation, integration, usability, persistence, audit, and release-hardening milestone.

Purpose:

Transform the collection of tools into a unified collector workflow.

The collector should be able to answer:

* What do I own?
* What am I missing?
* What should I buy next?
* Why should I buy it?
* How does it improve my collection?
* What series should I focus on?
* What opportunities are highest priority?

without manually jumping between multiple tools.
