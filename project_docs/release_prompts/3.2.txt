Begin v3.2 development.

Roadmap Context:

v3.1 = Deal Hunter MVP
v3.2 = Deal Hunter Workflow Refinement

GOAL:

Improve Deal Hunter accuracy, usability, parsing, and reporting using deterministic local logic.

This is NOT live scraping.

Do not add:

* live website scraping
* browser automation
* eBay API calls
* live pricing
* market prediction
* image recognition
* automatic purchasing
* automatic collection mutation

---

1. DEAL HUNTER PARSER REFINEMENT

---

Improve deterministic parsing for listing titles/descriptions.

Focus on:

* Newfoundland coins
* Canadian silver
* 1859 Large Cent varieties
* 1973 Large Bust quarter
* 1926 Near 6 nickel
* Canadian banknotes
* chartered banknotes
* slab companies: ICCS, CCCS, PCGS, NGC, BCS, PMG
* grade words and grade abbreviations
* lot / group / bulk listings
* damaged / cleaned / holed / bent / environmental damage keywords

---

2. REALISTIC LISTING EDGE CASES

---

Improve handling of:

* vague titles
* multiple coins in one listing
* “estate lot” titles
* inflated grade language
* “rare” keyword abuse
* high shipping
* non-CAD ambiguity
* no grade stated
* raw vs certified listings
* banknote vs coin distinction

---

3. DEAL RISK FLAGS

---

Add or refine risk flags:

* HIGH_SHIPPING
* UNCLEAR_GRADE
* RAW_OVERGRADED
* LOT_LISTING
* POSSIBLE_DAMAGE
* UNCLEAR_CURRENCY
* NON_COLLECTION_RELEVANT
* NEEDS_MANUAL_REVIEW

Risk flags should affect recommendation and explanation.

---

4. SCORING REFINEMENT

---

Review and refine:

* priority score
* liquidity score
* collection-fit score
* risk score
* max rational bid/price
* BUY / WATCH / PASS / NEGOTIATE / REVIEW logic

Preserve existing v3.1 behavior unless tests show a clear defect.

Do not claim market-pricing accuracy.

---

5. EXPLAINABILITY IMPROVEMENT

---

Improve Deal Hunter result explanations.

Every listing should clearly show:

* why it was recommended
* why it was risky
* what made it collection-relevant
* counterargument before BUY
* manual review reasons if applicable
