PHASE 1
V3.9 PORTFOLIO PERFORMANCE
--------------------------

GOAL

Transform collection records, snapshots, and intelligence into portfolio-level insights.

This is NOT investment advice.

Do NOT implement:

* live pricing
* scraping
* APIs
* market forecasting
* automatic purchases
* collection mutation

---

1. PORTFOLIO PERFORMANCE ENGINE

---

Create:

PortfolioPerformanceEngine

Purpose:

Analyze collection growth, quality, and progress.

Integrate with:

* Collection Snapshot System
* Collection Intelligence
* Opportunity Engine
* Market Intelligence
* Series Tracker

---

2. COLLECTION GROWTH ANALYSIS

---

Create:

CollectionGrowthReport

Track:

* collection size
* estimated collection value
* silver holdings
* slab count
* banknote count
* Newfoundland count
* custom category counts

Compare snapshots where available.

---

3. ACQUISITION PERFORMANCE

---

Create:

AcquisitionPerformanceReport

Highlight:

* best acquisitions
* biggest upgrades
* highest collection impact
* strongest opportunity captures
* highest confidence purchases

Use deterministic local data only.

---

4. SERIES PROGRESS ANALYSIS

---

Create:

SeriesProgressReport

Show:

* completion percentages
* progress since last snapshot
* nearest completions
* neglected series
* strongest-performing series

Support existing series tracking framework.

5. BUDGET ALLOCATION ANALYSIS

---

Create:

BudgetAllocationReport

* Newfoundland

* banknotes
* slabs
* upgrades
* duplicates
* other categories

Purpose:

Understand collection focus.
