PHASE 4
COLLECTION RELEVANCE SUMMARY
============================

Create:

CollectionRelevanceSummary

Purpose:

Explain why the opportunity matters to THIS collection.

Include:

* collection relevance score
* relevance explanation
* collection goal advanced

Classify as one or more:

* Upgrade
* Collection Gap
* Want-List Match
* Same-Grade Duplicate
* Lower-Grade Duplicate
* Speculative Opportunity
* General Collection Fit
* Not Collection Relevant

Required output:

Every enriched candidate must explicitly explain:

* why it helps the collection
* why it does not help the collection
* what collection objective it advances

Examples:

* Newfoundland completion
* Canadian silver expansion
* 1859 variety target
* 1926 Near 6 target
* Canadian banknote target
* upgrade opportunity
* duplicate risk

DEFINITION OF DONE
==================

Collector Companion can automatically enrich live, imported, ranked, and opportunity candidates with deterministic local Market Intelligence, including:

* deal quality
* confidence
* fair-value evidence
* collection relevance
* risk warnings
* review escalations
* counterarguments

while preserving original recommendations, avoiding live pricing, and maintaining complete collector control.

The system must clearly answer:

"Is this a good deal?"

AND

"Why does this matter to my collection?"
