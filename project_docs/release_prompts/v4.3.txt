PHASE 1
MISSION
=======

Collector Companion should know what the collector is actively seeking.

The system should automatically identify:

* want-list matches
* watchlist matches
* upgrade opportunities
* collection-gap opportunities
* high-priority collection targets

Do NOT add:

* push notifications
* email notifications
* SMS notifications
* background polling
* scheduled jobs
* automatic purchasing
* collection mutation

Alerts remain report-driven and user-triggered.
