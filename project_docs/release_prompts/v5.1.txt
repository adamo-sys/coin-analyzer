v5.1 = Phone Photo Capture

MISSION

Allow Collector Companion to accept and manage phone-captured coin and banknote photos through a mobile-style workflow.

This release is NOT:

* image recognition
* attribution
* grading
* OCR identification
* cloud sync
* automatic collection entry

Those arrive later.

v5.1 establishes photo intake and photo workflow architecture.
