PHASE 1
V3.3 OPPORTUNITY ENGINE
-----------------------

Roadmap Context:

v3.3 = Opportunity Engine

GOAL:

Answer:

"What should I buy next?"

using existing collection intelligence.

Do not add:

* scraping
* browser automation
* APIs
* live pricing
* market prediction
* image recognition
* automatic purchasing
* collection mutation

Reuse existing systems.

---

1. OPPORTUNITY ENGINE

---

Create:

OpportunityEngine

Purpose:

Identify the highest-impact collection opportunities.

Use:

* Collection Intelligence
* Smart Shopping Assistant
* Acquisition Impact
* Collection Quality
* Series Tracker
* Want List
* Deal Hunter
* Snapshot data where useful

---

* Newfoundland Opportunity

---
