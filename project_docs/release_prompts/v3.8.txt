PHASE 1
V3.8 MARKET INTELLIGENCE
------------------------

GOAL

Help answer:

"Is this actually a good deal?"

not just:

"Should I look at it?"

Do NOT implement:

* scraping
* browser automation
* eBay APIs
* dealer APIs
* auction APIs
* live pricing retrieval
* automatic purchasing
* collection mutation

All intelligence must remain deterministic and local.

---

1. MARKET INTELLIGENCE ENGINE

---

Create:

MarketIntelligenceEngine

Purpose:

Evaluate deal quality using available local information.

Integrate with:

* Deal Hunter
* Opportunity Engine
* Ranking Engine
* Collection Intelligence

---

2. FAIR VALUE ESTIMATES

---

Create:

FairValueEstimate

Support:

* Conservative Value
* Expected Value
* Aggressive Value

These are estimation bands only.

No market-price guarantees.

No live pricing claims.

---

3. DEAL QUALITY MODEL

---

Create:

DealQuality

Possible outputs:

* Excellent
* Good
* Fair
* Weak
* Overpriced
* Unknown

Reasoning required.

---

4. OPPORTUNITY CONFIDENCE

---

Create:

OpportunityConfidence

Score:

0–100

Based on:

* collection fit
* duplicate status
* upgrade potential
* gap impact
* data completeness
* risk indicators
* available valuation evidence

Deterministic only.

---

5. COMPARABLE SALES FRAMEWORK

---

Create:

ComparableSale

Support local/manual data only.

Examples:

* auction result
* dealer observation
* user-entered comp
* catalog value
* historical reference

No web retrieval.

No API calls.

---

6. VALUE EXPLANATION

---

Create:

MarketIntelligenceReport

Include:

* deal quality
* confidence score
* fair value range
* strengths
* weaknesses
* risk factors
* counterargument
* buy rationale (if applicable)

Every recommendation must include a counterargument.

7. RISK ANALYSIS

---

* high shipping
* vague title
* uncertain attribution
* raw grade risk
* damaged/cleaned risk
* low-information listing
* duplicate ownership
* weak collection relevance

Output:

RiskSummary
