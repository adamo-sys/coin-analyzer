# v7.3 Acquisition Strategy

## Summary

Acquisition Strategy orchestrates existing collection intelligence, insights, analytics, opportunity scoring, and market intelligence into strategic acquisition plans with phased priorities, portfolio balance guidance, and risk-adjusted recommendations.

This is deterministic, explainable, and derived only from local collection data. No AI reasoning, forecasting, machine learning, or external APIs.

## What's New

- `AcquisitionStrategyEngine` - main orchestrator for strategic acquisition planning
- `AcquisitionPriority` - single prioritized acquisition target with category, risk, budget guidance, and confidence
- `AcquisitionPhase` - phased strategic plan with timeframe, targets, estimated budget, and expected outcomes
- `PortfolioBalanceRecommendation` - category-level balance guidance with current vs. recommended percentages
- `RiskAssessment` - overall risk level with risk factors, mitigation strategies, and market risk notes
- `AcquisitionStrategyReport` - complete strategy report with overview, context, plan, priorities, balance, risk, and actions
- `StrategyDashboard` - dashboard summary with priority counts, category breakdown, and total estimated budget
- Strategic plan phases: Immediate (next 1-3 purchases), Short-Term (3-6 months), Long-Term (6-12 months)
- Priority categories: Series Completion, Upgrade, WANT_LIST, Gap Fill, Diversification, Key Date, Budget Opportunity
- Portfolio balance analysis for Newfoundland Coinage, Canadian Silver, 1859 Large Cent Varieties, and Geographic Diversification
- Risk assessment with LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH classification and mitigation strategies
- Export support: Markdown and CSV for strategy reports and priorities
- GUI integration: Tools -> Acquisition Strategy dialog with 7 tabs (Dashboard, Strategy Overview, Immediate Priorities, Short-Term Priorities, Long-Term Priorities, Portfolio Balance, Risk Assessment)

## Architecture

The Acquisition Strategy Engine extends existing platform capabilities:

- Reuses Collection Insights data for collection context and observations
- Reuses Platform Analytics for subsystem health and metrics
- Reuses Collection Intelligence for duplicate/upgrade/gap detection
- Reuses Opportunity Engine for upgrade opportunity identification
- Reuses Series Tracker for series completion analysis
- Reuses Portfolio Performance for portfolio balance and category analysis
- Reuses Market Intelligence for deal quality and risk assessment context

The engine does not create new decision logic. It orchestrates and extends existing engines into a higher-level strategic planning layer.

## Limitations

- Deterministic local guidance only. No scraping, APIs, live pricing, market forecasting, automatic purchasing, or collection mutation.
- Budget guidance is internal estimation from text parsing, not live market pricing.
- Strategy recommendations should be reviewed before any purchase decisions.
- Series data and opportunity data are optional; the engine generates reasonable defaults from collection data alone.
- Portfolio balance percentages are based on item counts, not market value.
