# Coin Analyzer v3.2

## Overview

v3.2 is the Deal Hunter Workflow Refinement release.

It improves the offline eBay.ca-style Deal Hunter workflow without adding scraping, browser automation, eBay API usage, live listing fetches, live market pricing, market prediction, automatic purchasing, image recognition, or collection mutation.

## Major Changes

- Expanded deterministic parsing for Newfoundland, Canadian silver, 1859 Large Cents, 1973 Large Bust quarters, 1926 Near 6 nickels, Canadian/chartered banknotes, slab companies, grade words, lot listings, and problem-coin language.
- Added risk flags for high shipping, unclear grade, raw-overgraded language, lot listings, possible damage, unclear currency, non-collection relevance, and manual-review needs.
- Hardened CSV import with safe column aliases, missing optional-column handling, malformed price warnings, skipped-row reporting, and extra-column tolerance.
- Improved GUI CSV import feedback with rows found, listings importable, skipped rows, and warnings.
- Expanded CSV and Markdown exports with parsed candidate fields, total cost, scores, recommendation, risk flags, counterargument, reasons, warnings, and listing URL.
- Expanded the sample Deal Hunter CSV fixture under `test_data/deal_hunter/`.

## Testing and Quality

## Known Limitations

- Deal Hunter remains deterministic local guidance only.
- Max rational price is not live market pricing, appraisal, or a guarantee of value.
- CSV/listing parsing is intentionally conservative and should be manually reviewed for ambiguous, bulk, problem, or non-CAD listings.
- URLs and image URLs are stored as reference data only.
