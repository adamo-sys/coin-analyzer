# Coin Analyzer v3.8

## Overview

Coin Analyzer v3.8 adds Market Intelligence: a deterministic local analysis layer that explains value, confidence, risk, and deal quality before future live listing ingestion begins.

This release does not add scraping, browser automation, APIs, live pricing, automatic purchasing, image recognition, or collection mutation.

## Major Features

- `MarketIntelligenceEngine` for local value and risk analysis of supplied listings.
- `FairValueEstimate` with conservative, expected, and aggressive value bands.
- `ComparableSale` support from manually supplied comps and local Market Awareness observations.
- `DealQuality` labels: Excellent, Good, Fair, Weak, Overpriced, or Unknown.
- `OpportunityConfidence` scoring from collection fit, duplicate risk, upgrade/gap impact, data completeness, valuation evidence, and risk penalties.
- `RiskSummary` with clear warnings and severity.
- Collector-facing counterargument and buy rationale.
- Tools -> Market Intelligence GUI workflow.
- CSV and Markdown export.

## Architecture Highlights

Market Intelligence reuses existing systems instead of creating duplicate recommendation logic:

- Deal Hunter for listing parsing, risk flags, recommendation, and max rational price.
- Opportunity Engine for opportunity context.
- Collection Intelligence for owned/duplicate/upgrade/gap signals.
- WANT_LIST context for explicit target recognition.
- Market Awareness for local comparable sale and observed-price evidence.

## Known Limitations

- Fair-value estimates are deterministic local guidance only.
- Estimates are not appraisals, live market pricing, predictions, or guarantees of value.
- Comparable-sale quality depends on local Market Awareness records and user-supplied comparable data.
- The GUI supports manual listing entry only; it does not fetch listings or browse URLs.
