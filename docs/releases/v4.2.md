# Coin Analyzer v4.2

## Overview

v4.2 adds Market Intelligence Automation.

The release automatically enriches live, imported, ranked, and manually entered candidate listings with existing deterministic local Market Intelligence. It answers both:

- Is this a good deal?
- Why does this matter to my collection?

## Major Features

- `market_intelligence_automation.py`
- `MarketIntelligenceAutomationEngine`
- `MarketEnrichedCandidate`
- `MarketEnrichmentBatchReport`
- `FairValueEvidenceSummary`
- `CollectionRelevanceSummary`
- Single candidate enrichment
- CandidatePool enrichment
- Ranking report enrichment
- Live Deal Hunter report enrichment
- Tools -> Market Intelligence Automation
- CSV and Markdown export

## Enrichment Workflow

Candidate source
-> existing Market Intelligence
-> fair-value evidence summary
-> collection relevance summary
-> risk and confidence review
-> enriched report

Original recommendations are preserved. If confidence is low, risk is high, or evidence is weak, the enriched recommendation can escalate to REVIEW with an explicit reason.

## Collection Relevance Model

Candidates can be classified as:

- Upgrade
- Collection Gap
- Want-List Match
- Same-Grade Duplicate
- Lower-Grade Duplicate
- Speculative Opportunity
- General Collection Fit
- Not Collection Relevant

The report explains what collection objective is advanced, why the candidate helps, and why it may not help.

## Fair-Value Evidence Summary

Evidence summaries include:

- Comparable sales count
- Market Awareness records used
- Evidence quality
- Evidence gaps
- Confidence impact

No live price retrieval, exchange-rate lookup, or market forecasting is performed.

## Known Limitations

- Deterministic local guidance only.
- No scraping.
- No APIs.
- No live pricing.
- No exchange-rate lookup.
- No market forecasting.
- No investment advice.
- No bidding or purchasing.
- No collection mutation.
