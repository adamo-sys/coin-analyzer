# Coin Analyzer v2.0

Release date: 2026-06-18

Release hash: `a661b06c846bdd0d5342ce892c350832c8907974`

## Overview

Coin Analyzer v2.0 is the Collector Operating System milestone. It consolidates the v1.x toolset into a unified collector workflow without adding scraping, live pricing, OCR, image recognition, AI grading, or external market integrations.

The release is focused on answering the practical collector questions in one place:

- What do I own?
- What am I missing?
- What should I buy next?
- Why should I buy it?
- How does it improve my collection?
- What series should I focus on?
- Which opportunities are highest priority?

## Major Features

- Collector Home: unified Tools menu entry that displays collection summary, best next purchase, highest-impact opportunity, top WANT_LIST target, series closest to completion, quality score, recent market activity, photo coverage, and suggested workflow steps.
- Collection Health Report: consolidated report combining Collection Dashboard, Collection Quality, Series Tracker, Smart Shopping Assistant, Market Awareness, Photo Vault coverage, strengths, weaknesses, priorities, recommended actions, and persistence findings.
- Export support: Collector Home and Collection Health Report both export CSV and Markdown.
- Workflow consolidation: the recommended flow is Collector Home -> Listing Analyzer -> Acquisition Impact -> Smart Shopping Assistant -> Photo Vault -> Market Awareness -> Dashboard -> Health Report.
- Persistence audit: documents what survives restart and what remains runtime/local supplied state.

## Architecture Highlights

- `collector_operating_system.py` composes existing engines instead of duplicating ownership, duplicate, upgrade, acquisition, shopping, series, quality, market, or photo logic.
- `coin_collection_gui.py` adds Tools -> Collector Home and Tools -> Collection Health Report.
- `test_collector_operating_system.py` covers consolidated report generation, Smart Shopping integration, workflow guidance, export consistency, persistence findings, empty collection behavior, and photo/market context.

## Known Limitations

- Collector Home and Collection Health Report are consolidation/reporting layers, not new decision engines.
- Shared Session Context is per session and does not persist after restart.
- Market Awareness, Photo Vault, and Smart Shopping candidate records are runtime/local supplied structures unless a future persistence layer is added.
- Listing Analyzer still stores URLs only and does not fetch or scrape them.
- No live pricing, market prediction, OCR, image recognition, AI grading, scraping, Numista expansion, or market-price APIs.
