# Coin Analyzer v3.3

## Overview

v3.3 adds the Opportunity Engine, a deterministic local planning layer that answers:

"What should I buy next?"

It uses existing collection intelligence and shopping systems to identify high-impact collection opportunities without scraping, APIs, live pricing, market prediction, image recognition, automatic purchasing, or collection mutation.

## Major Features

- Opportunity Engine for budget-aware collection opportunity planning.
- Opportunity types:
  - Upgrade Opportunity
  - Collection Gap Opportunity
  - Series Completion Opportunity
  - Want List Opportunity
  - Newfoundland Opportunity
  - Canadian Silver Opportunity
  - Canadian Banknote Opportunity
  - High-ROI Opportunity
- Budget scenarios for $50, $100, $250, and $500.
- OpportunityScore with collection fit, upgrade impact, completion impact, liquidity, risk, and collection-priority components.
- OpportunityReport rows with reasoning, risks, counterargument, estimated collection impact, budget fit, source, and recommendation.
- TopOpportunitiesReport sections for top overall, under $100, Newfoundland, banknote, and upgrade opportunities.
- Deal Hunter result support as opportunity inputs.
- Workflows -> Opportunity Engine GUI entry point.
- CSV and Markdown export.

## Architecture

The engine reuses:

- Collection Intelligence
- Smart Shopping Assistant
- Acquisition Impact
- Collection Quality
- Series Tracker
- WANT_LIST context
- Deal Hunter
- Local Market Awareness

It does not duplicate owned, duplicate, upgrade, or acquisition-impact classification logic.

## Known Limitations

- Opportunity scoring is deterministic local guidance only.
- Unpriced opportunities require manual budget review.
- No live market-pricing accuracy is claimed.
- The engine ranks supplied/generated local opportunities only.
- It does not scrape, fetch listing pages, call APIs, predict markets, buy automatically, recognize images, or modify collection data.
