# Third-Party Notices

Coin Analyzer depends on third-party software, services, data sources, APIs, and development tools. Those materials remain subject to their own licences and terms.

## Dependency source of truth

The project's dependency manifests and lockfiles are the primary machine-readable inventory. This document is a human-readable compliance record and should be updated when a dependency or external asset requires attribution, redistribution notices, bundled licence text, source-offer obligations, or other special handling.

## Current compliance rule

Before adding or distributing a third-party component, dataset, model, image, font, API-derived dataset, or other external asset:

1. identify its owner/provider;
2. record the applicable licence or terms;
3. confirm the intended use is permitted;
4. confirm redistribution rights if the material will ship with Coin Analyzer;
5. preserve required copyright and attribution notices;
6. document any source-code, notice-file, patent, trademark, or reciprocal-licensing obligations;
7. keep restricted or confidential material outside the public repository.
