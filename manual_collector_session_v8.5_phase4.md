## 5. Workflow Friction

### Too many clicks / mental context switching
- **Problem:** The workspace has 11 panels. A collector has to browse all 11 to understand their collection state.
- **Impact:** High cognitive load. No single "what should I do today?" view.
- **Evidence:** I had to call 11 separate methods to get a complete picture. In the GUI, that's 11 tabs or menu items.

### Confusing labels / weak evidence
- **Problem:** "Acquire Canada 50 cents" with no year is not actionable.
- **Impact:** The collector doesn't know which specific coin to buy.
- **Evidence:** Gap targets show `year: None` for many items because the gap detector doesn't know what year to recommend.

### Missing connections between panels
- **Problem:** Workflow shows 6 pending reviews but Inbox shows 0.
- **Impact:** The collector doesn't know where to find pending reviews.
- **Evidence:** `workflow_status.pending_reviews: 6` vs `inbox.total_pending: 0`.

### Photo coverage completely ignored
- **Problem:** 41 items without photos, 0% coverage, but Advisor says nothing.
- **Impact:** Photos are required for grading, selling, and insurance. This is a blind spot.
- **Evidence:** `photo_vault.items_without_photos: 41` but `advisor.recommendations: 0` for grade submission. The photo vault audit reports `missing_photo_count: 0` which is incorrect.

### Duplicate threshold too high
- **Problem:** Duplicate disposal only triggers at 50+ items. With 41 items and 4 duplicate pairs, no disposal recommendation is made.
- **Impact:** The collector has duplicate coins but isn't told to review them.
- **Evidence:** `summary.total_items: 41` (just below 50 threshold) with 4 actual duplicate pairs.

### Opportunities panel requires external data
- **Problem:** The opportunity engine produces nothing without shopping candidates.
- **Impact:** Intrinsic opportunities (want-list gaps, upgrade targets) aren't surfaced as opportunities.
- **Evidence:** `opportunities.total_opportunities: 0` despite `want_list.total_gaps: 32`.

### Overwhelming gap count
- **Problem:** 32 gaps for 41 items is too many to be actionable.
- **Impact:** The collector feels overwhelmed and ignores the gap list.
- **Evidence:** `want_list.total_gaps: 32`. Many gaps are for obscure years the collector doesn't care about.

### Generic top priority

## Summary

The v8.5 Advisor is **architecturally sound** but **operationally immature**. The Phase 3 enrichment works (every recommendation has 3 evidence items), but the underlying intelligence engines are producing:

- **Too many gaps** (32 for 41 items)
- **No upgrade candidates** (despite 4 duplicate pairs)
- **No photo recommendations** (due to a counting bug)
- **No opportunities** (without external shopping data)
- **Generic priorities** ("Back up collection data" when already backed up)

### The highest-value Phase 4 work

1. **Fix the photo vault missing_photo_count bug** — this alone would add 1-2 meaningful recommendations.
2. **Improve gap quality** — filter or rank gaps so the top 3 are genuinely actionable (with specific years, not "None").
3. **Detect upgrade candidates from duplicates** — the upgrade advisor should use duplicate pairs as upgrade sources.
4. **Lower or replace the duplicate disposal threshold** — base it on actual duplicate count, not total collection size.
5. **Generate intrinsic opportunities** — use want-list gaps and upgrade targets as opportunities even without external shopping data.
6. **Add a "Today's Focus" summary** — one view that synthesizes the most important 3-5 actions from all panels.

These are all **improvements to existing intelligence**, not new engines or new recommendation categories. They fit within the category freeze.
