# Desktop Acceptance v1 — Review & Evidence Worksheet

Status: **pre-photography / unresolved**

This worksheet operationalizes the merged desktop-acceptance review/provenance protocol for the finalized 30-case roster. It is a human-review work queue, not benchmark ground truth and not authorization to run recognition.

## Rules

- Ground-truth (GT) review and expected-action review are independent decisions.
- Each requires two independent reviewer records with evidence references.
- Do not use Coin Analyzer, Terra, another recognition model, or benchmark output as evidence for GT or expected action.
- A reviewer disagreement remains unresolved until a distinct adjudicator records evidence and rationale.
- Privacy, licensing, and provider authorization remain unresolved until evidence supporting each approval exists.
- Candidate identity and candidate expected action are prompts for review, not completed review decisions.
- Official benchmark photography does not begin from this worksheet. Capture and near-duplicate review remain later phases.
- Repeated cases 028–030 reuse specimens 006, 011, and 012 respectively. Their GT/provenance evidence may reference the same physical specimen, but each case still requires the review state demanded by the authoring contract.

## Provenance work queue

The roster currently describes each specimen as a user-owned physical specimen and uses `inventory:Sxxx` evidence references. Those declarations are candidate provenance records, not automatic provider approvals.

For each distinct specimen (001–027), establish evidence sufficient to support:

1. **Physical-specimen linkage** — evidence that the reviewed coin corresponds to the inventory specimen ID.
2. **Image-rights basis** — evidence supporting the right to create/use the eventual benchmark photographs.
3. **Privacy review** — evidence that the planned images contain no disallowed/private content or that any required handling has been approved.
4. **Licensing review** — evidence supporting benchmark use of the planned image bytes.
5. **Provider authorization review** — evidence that the eventual benchmark image use is permitted for the selected provider path.

Until those records exist, every provider-eligibility state remains `unresolved`.

## Completion gates before photography

Photography may begin only after the project intentionally accepts the reviewed roster state and has reviewer assignments plus provenance/authorization evidence organized for the cases being captured. The authoring plan itself must remain fail-closed: no review or approval field is changed to `complete`/`approved` merely because this worksheet exists.

Recognition remains prohibited on candidate benchmark captures until the accepted image bytes have been frozen and validated under the strict manifest contract.
